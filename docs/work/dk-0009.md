## Scope

`message retire-spool --recipient ROLE_OR_UUID [--dry-run]` retires unclaimed Claude spool
entries of terminal messages for one conversation.

## Criteria

- It holds the generation lease, then `unblock.lock`, as the consumer does.
- An entry is retired only when its receipt matches the entry at `sending` and exactly one
  message record holds its request ID, is `handled` or `superseded`, and binds the same Claude
  recipient and transport text.
- Retired entries move by rename, with identical bytes, to `unblock/claude-retired/`. Receipts
  stay at their bound path and nothing is deleted.
- Every other entry is retained with a reason. Output reports exact retired and retained counts.
- `--dry-run` reports the same outcomes and moves nothing. A lock held for 60 seconds fails with
  `spool-busy`.

## Decisions

- Each recipient's owner runs the command for its own spool.
