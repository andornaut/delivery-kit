## Scope

`inbox snapshot` and `inbox ack` accept a registered route target whose selected harness is
Claude as the recipient, in addition to the active route's recipient.

## Criteria

- A route target's snapshot and acknowledgement cover only its own delivered entries and
  retentions, with no peer or target rows.
- A route target's snapshot accepts no other conversation as a parent.
- Any other Claude recipient is refused.

## Decisions

- A route target reconciles only its own channel deliveries, so it never sees or acknowledges
  another role's entries.

## Outcome
