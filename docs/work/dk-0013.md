## Scope

The observer's wake time, and the contract diagnostics for a missing review slot.

## Criteria

- The observer sleeps until the start of the minute after its pass's own clock reading, and at
  most 60 seconds when the clock steps back.
- A pass that runs past a minute boundary still observes the minute that began during it. A slot
  whose whole minute passes during one pass is skipped, not replayed.
- The contract review-slot wait fails within its own 45 second deadline, stops when the runner
  exits, and names the slot, the virtual time and the runner's last output lines.

## Decisions

- Fixed in the observer's wake computation, not by raising contract timeouts.

Shipped in 4.6.2 (14c59c8, 91c16c1). See [runner](../reference/runner.md).
