## Scope

Contract fixtures start the runner without consuming virtual time before its first observer
pass.

## Criteria

- `startRunner` exposes `armed()`, which completes after the runner's first resource sample,
  after the observer's first pass and sleep.
- `startRunnerAt` holds the virtual clock at its epoch until the runner is armed.
- The tick, health, review schedule, release tick and Claude event cases use it; the late-start
  case still arms the runner 30 seconds after its slot.

## Decisions

- The observer never replays a slot that began before its first clock reading, so fixtures arm
  the runner before the slot instead of lengthening timeouts.

## Outcome

Shipped in 4.6.1 (0d36b0c). Test fixtures only; no runtime change.
