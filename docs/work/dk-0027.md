## Scope

The observer's `scheduled-status` detail says a departure is still checking or finishing whenever
`bus/active-departure` is non-empty, including when the departure is the one this slot started.
Release delivery-kit 6.1.1.

## Criteria

- The observer compares the start epoch recorded in `bus/active-departure` with the slot start.
  A departure that started at or after the slot is reported as this slot's own; one that started
  earlier, or an unreadable epoch, keeps the still-checking text.
- The deploy-pending and idle texts are unchanged.
- The event identity is unchanged: type `scheduled-status`, kind `runner`, key the slot.
