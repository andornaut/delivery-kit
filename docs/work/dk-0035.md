## Scope

The scheduled-slot report for the slot's own departure reads "this slot's departure is
running", which also appears when that departure then finds nothing new on main and does
nothing. Change the text to "this slot's departure is checking main; any attempt is reported
separately." Event identity and selection are unchanged. Ships with the next delivery-kit release,
not alone.

## Criteria

- The own-slot text is the new wording; the earlier-start, deploy-pending and idle texts are
  unchanged.
- Unit and runner-level race tests assert the new text.
