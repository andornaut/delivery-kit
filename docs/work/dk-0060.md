## Scope

Negotiate the coordinator's selector from its installed CLI version. Keep
the older subprocess contract for compatible legacy readers and preserve
Delivery Kit's own selectors, bus identity, schedules and admission.

## References

- [Runner reference](../reference/runner.md#coordinator-cli-seam)
- [Coordination Kit ck-0084](https://github.com/andornaut/coordination-kit/blob/main/docs/work/ck-0084.md)

## Verification

- Verify the old reader contract, app ID validation, executable replacement
  and refusal without retrying a different selector.
- Exercise the exact published 8.0.0 reader and the checked alias-free 9.0.0
  candidate. Verify legacy loading and the new workspace layout with the
  existing delivery bus path before rollout.

## Outcome

Delivery Kit 8.8 bundles Coordination Kit 9.0.0 and negotiates the installed
reader's selector and response identity together. The public dependency
matches the qualified runtime. The runner-first preflight exercised real
review and ingest with 8.0.0 and 9.0.0 through the legacy and canonical
workspace layouts. It preserved the bus path, pause, event prefix and cursor.
Game Coordinator owns live adoption.
