## Scope

Use app terminology at public boundaries, with a compatible reader for legacy
commands, configuration and events. Use registered operator authority and
configured routing without requiring particular role names. Keep the existing
state paths and preserve configured schedules and persisted delivery records.

## Criteria

- Qualify solo and multi-app configurations, permission denials, and scheduled
  and ad hoc delivery without fixed role names.
- Qualify mixed-version event and review ingestion and existing workspace
  continuity against the exact compatible Coordination Kit.
- Publish checked Delivery Kit after the compatible Coordination Kit reader.
