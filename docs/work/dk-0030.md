## Scope

Reduce the executed test cases by 20 percent from a baseline taken when the work starts, with
each removal justified by retained coverage. This target replaces an earlier 40 percent target,
whose pruning shipped in 4.2.0.

## Criteria

- A recorded baseline case count from the current suite.
- At most 80 percent of the baseline cases remain, and every removal names the case that keeps
  its coverage.
- `make check` passes.

## Decisions

- The baseline is taken at the start, because the suite has been restructured since the request
  (5.0 extraction, 6.x and 7.0).

## Outcome

Not started.
