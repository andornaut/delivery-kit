## Scope

Dispatch to a Claude spool held by another sender or by the channel consumer.

## Criteria

- The attempt is refused before any receipt or spool entry is written.
- The message is `blocked` with reason `transport-busy` and a `busy` diagnostic
  (classification `unavailable`, stage `receipt`).
- A later `message dispatch` starts a new guarded attempt.

## Decisions

- A busy spool is transient, so it is treated like `transport-unavailable` rather than as an
  undiagnosed refusal.

## Outcome

Shipped in 4.6.2 (4d9f262). See [Common messages](../reference/messages.md).
