## Scope

One protocol for durable linked requests, work and messages: role-owned canonical document trees
outside worktrees in a dedicated local state root, sparse derived indexes, fenced atomic writers,
and immutable evidence and history, folded in before the first 4.5 adoption.

## Criteria

- Canonical records live in an external state root, not in source repositories or worktrees.
- Writers are fenced and atomic; evidence and history are immutable.
- It ships before the first 4.5 adoption.

## Decisions

- Filed with this convention after delivery; the approval predates the work-file convention.

## Outcome

Shipped in 4.5.0. The work protocol moved to coordination-kit in 5.0 ([dk-0018](dk-0018.md)).
