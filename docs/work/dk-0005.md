## Scope

The Claude adapter spools a brief or event while `claude agents --json` lists the recipient as
an interactive session with status `idle`, `busy`, `shell` or `waiting` and no `state`.

## Criteria

- A missing or blocked session, or any other reported status or state, defers delivery.
- The deferral reason names the reported token, or `unknown` when it is not a plain lowercase
  token.

## Decisions

- `shell` and `waiting` are treated like `busy`: the brief queues in the channel until the
  session reads it.
- Naming the token makes an unrecognized status diagnosable from the deferral alone.
