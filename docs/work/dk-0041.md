## Scope

Keep the exact scheduled review slot after Coordination Kit explicitly defers it
for an active compaction reservation. Retry it through the resident observer.

## Criteria

- Retry the original epoch in an existing observer pass after the reservation ends.
- Keep new due slots distinct and preserve the handling of ambiguous outcomes and
  the event ingest cursor.
- Qualify the released seam with the compaction-aware Coordination Kit.

## Decisions

## Outcome

The resident observer retries an explicitly deferred slot until the reservation
clears or it receives another outcome. The built runner contract verifies the
original epoch, fresh-slot priority, ingest and terminal outcomes.
